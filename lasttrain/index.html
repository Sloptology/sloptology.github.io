LAST TRAIN HOME

A story from the space between stations.

Submitted for your consideration: Walter Ames, 52, a salesman of reading lamps. Tonight he wants one small thing, his own front door and the porch light his wife Marian leaves burning. He has stepped onto the 11:47. It is not on any timetable.

GOAL: find the 6 clues hidden in the stations, then get Walter home.
1. Move your light (mouse or finger). Click the objects it shows.
2. Use the timetable at the bottom. When a train says Doors open, press Board.
3. A figure follows you and gets closer with every ride and every minute you linger. Do not stare at it: holding your gaze makes it move.
4. Keep your Mind bar up. Red shortcut trains save time but cost more.
