The Room Remembers

You wake on a bed in a room you do not know. There is a door. It is locked.

Look around. Turn with the buttons or the arrow keys. Click what you want to touch. The Objective line above the room tells you what to do next.

Four chapters. Whatever you look at, touch, or ignore is kept. Headphones help. The Journal keeps what you learn, because the room will not.
